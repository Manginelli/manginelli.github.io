DEMON HUNTER

An endless war against demons, within and without.

Ten thousand years ago, a group of Night Elves made a solemn pact to turn the Burning Legion’s own power against it. Through a profane ritual of demon blood, they sacrificed their souls to keep demons at bay for millennia, scorned by the very people they protected.

However, everything changed after the Third War. The Night Elves lost their immortality, and so did the Demon Hunters. Demetrius Darkheart, the last of the original Pact, desperately trains a new generation before the next invasion comes. Even if that means sharing the Pact’s secrets with the orcs.

You are one of them. Burning blood runs through your veins, and the demon within whispers, bargains and screams, demanding to be freed. Steady your blade and your mind, for the hunt is never over.

Martial Arts Work in Progress 37

Work in Progress 38

Key Spells & Abilities

  • Double Strike

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Single Target
    • Melee Range
    • Physical Damage
    • 8 sec cooldown
    • Requires a main hand and an off-hand weapon
    • In the stage

    Strikes twice in quick succession, first with your main hand and then with your off-hand weapon. Each strike causes 64% weapon damage plus a small amount of additional damage.

  • Dive

    • Instant
    • Very Low Mana
    • Single Target
    • 7 to 20 yds
    • Physical Damage
    • 30 sec cooldown
    • In the stage

    Dives at the target and lands on its other side, causing a small amount of damage.

  • Blade Dash

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Self
    • Physical Damage
    • 12 sec cooldown
    • Requires Melee Weapon
    • In the stage

    Dashes 7 yds forward, striking all enemies in your path for 26% of your main hand and off-hand weapon damage plus a moderate amount of additional damage. Cannot miss, be dodged or be parried.

  • Wind Walk

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Self
    • 30 sec cooldown
    • In the stage

    Increases your movement speed by 40% and your chance to dodge attacks and spells by 40% for 5 sec. Using any ability or attacking ends the effect.

  • Battle Rhythm

    • Passive
    • In the stage

    Each of your melee weapon hits that lands reduces the remaining cooldown of your Double Strike, Mana Strike and Flame Strike by 0.5 sec.

  • The Hunt

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Single Target
    • 45 yds
    • Physical Damage
    • 3 min cooldown
    • Requires Melee Weapon
    • In the stage

    Leaps at the target and stuns it for 1 sec. During the stun, you make 5 extra melee attacks, alternating between your main hand and off-hand weapons.

Key Talents

  • Improved Reflexes Tier 1 · 5 ranks

    Increases your chance to dodge by 1/2/3/4/5%.

  • Multistrike Tier 1 · 5 ranks

    Each hit of your Double Strike has a 1/2/3/4/5% chance to make it strike again 0.5 sec later for 50% of its damage, at no mana cost. Cannot trigger itself.

  • Dual Wield Mastery Tier 2 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 5 points in Martial Arts Talents

    Increases the damage done by your off-hand weapon by 10/20/30/40/50%.

  • Eternal Pursuit Tier 2 · 2 ranks

    • Requires 5 points in Martial Arts Talents

    Reduces the duration of movement impairing and fear effects on you by 20/40%.

  • Improved Blade Dash Tier 2 · 2 ranks

    • Requires 5 points in Martial Arts Talents
    • In the stage

    Reduces the cooldown of your Blade Dash by 2/4 sec.

  • Friend or Foe Tier 3 · 1 rank

    • Requires 10 points in Martial Arts Talents

    Your Dive can now also be used on friendly targets, causing no damage, and its cooldown is reduced by 5 sec.

  • Muscle Memory Tier 3 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 10 points in Martial Arts Talents
    • In the stage

    Gives your melee auto attacks a 2/4/6/8/10% chance to make your next ability cost no mana. That ability does not interrupt your mana regeneration.

  • Battle Trance Tier 4 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 15 points in Martial Arts Talents

    Each of your melee auto attacks, Double Strike hits and Blade Dash hits that lands increases your haste and movement speed by 1%, stacking up to 2/4/6/8/10 times. One stack is lost for every 2.5 sec without landing a melee attack.

  • Fervor of the Hunt Tier 4 · 1 rank

    • Requires 15 points in Martial Arts Talents

    Your relentless pace inspires your allies, increasing the melee, ranged and spellcasting haste of all party members within 45 yards by 3%.

  • Momentum Tier 5 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 20 points in Martial Arts Talents
    • In the stage

    Each hit of your Double Strike that lands grants you a stack of Momentum, increasing your movement speed by 0.5/1/1.5/2/2.5%. Stacks up to 4 times. Lasts 30 sec. Your Blade Dash consumes up to 4 stacks of Momentum, increasing its damage by 20% for each stack consumed.

  • The Hunt Tier 5 · 1 rank

    • Requires 20 points in Martial Arts Talents
    • In the stage

    Teaches The Hunt.

  • Hunt Prey Tier 7 · 1 rank

    • Requires 30 points in Martial Arts Talents
    • Requires 5 points in Momentum
    • In the stage

    When your Blade Dash consumes 4 stacks of Momentum, the cooldown of your Dive is reset and your next Dive within 5 sec has no minimum range, costs no mana, deals 500% additional damage and does not trigger the global cooldown.

Spellbreaker Work in Progress 39

Work in Progress 40

Key Spells & Abilities

  • Mana Strike

    • Instant
    • High Mana
    • Single Target
    • Melee Range
    • Arcane Damage
    • 8 sec cooldown
    • Requires Melee Weapon
    • In the stage

    An instant strike that causes Arcane damage equal to 3 times your main hand weapon's damage per second. If the target has mana, also burns mana equal to 100% of the damage dealt and causes 50% of that amount as additional Arcane damage.

  • Disrupt

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Single Target
    • 10 yds
    • Arcane Damage
    • 15 sec cooldown
    • In the stage

    Interrupts the target's spellcasting, burning mana equal to the interrupted spell's cost and causing Arcane damage equal to 50% of the mana burned. Prevents any spell in that school from being cast for 4 sec.

  • Shackle Demon

    • Cast
    • High Mana
    • Single Target
    • 25 yds
    • 15 sec cooldown
    • In the stage

    Shackles the target demon for up to 30 sec. The shackled demon is unable to move, attack or cast spells, and any spell it is casting is interrupted. You stop attacking the target until you click on it or attack it again. Any damage caused will break the effect. Only one target can be shackled at a time.

  • Mana Torrent

    • Instant
    • Very High Mana
    • All enemies within 20 yds
    • 2 min cooldown

    Releases a torrent of raw mana, silencing all enemies within 20 yards for 2 sec.

  • Break Spell

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Self
    • 20 sec cooldown

    Surrounds you with a ward that negates the next spell cast on you and restores mana to you equal to the mana its caster spent on it. Lasts 5 sec.

  • Still Mind

    • Channeled
    • Self
    • 3 min cooldown
    • In the stage

    Try to focus your mind for 2 sec. While channeling, your mana regeneration is active and increased by 1400%.

  • Mirror Image

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Self
    • 5 min cooldown
    • In the stage

    Splits your form into 3 images of yourself for 15 sec, causing enemies to lose track of you and removing all your threat. Each image has 25% of your maximum health, starting at the same share of health as you. The images surround your target, attack it and use Mana Strike. If you have Mana Charge, each Mana Strike they land grants you a stack of it.

  • Mana Breaker

    • Instant
    • Very High Mana
    • Single Target
    • Melee Range
    • Arcane Damage
    • 12 sec cooldown
    • Requires Melee Weapon
    • In the stage

    Breaks the target's defenses with a blade of arcane energy, causing Arcane damage equal to 6.75 times your main hand weapon's damage per second and granting you 2 stacks of Mana Charge. If the target has mana, also burns mana equal to 100% of the damage dealt and causes 50% of that amount as additional Arcane damage.

  • Mana Scar

    • Cast
    • High Mana
    • Single Target
    • Melee Range
    • Arcane Damage
    • [cooldown]
    • Requires a main hand and an off-hand weapon
    • In the stage

    Gathers your focus, then cuts the target twice in quick succession with blades of mana, first with your main hand and then with your off-hand weapon. Each cut causes Arcane damage equal to 12.6 times that weapon's damage per second. Can only be used while you have 8 stacks of Mana Charge, and consumes them after the second cut.

Key Talents

  • Improved Mana Strike Tier 1 · 5 ranks

    Increases the damage of your Mana Strike by 2/4/6/8/10% and reduces its mana cost by 5/10/15/20/25%.

  • Willpower Tier 1 · 5 ranks

    Increases your Intellect and Spirit by 1/2/3/4/5%.

  • Improved Arcane Ink Tier 2 · 3 ranks

    • Requires 5 points in Spellbreaker Talents

    Reduces all magic damage you take by 2/4/6%.

  • Focused Mind Tier 2 · 3 ranks

    • Requires 5 points in Spellbreaker Talents

    Increases your chance to hit with all attacks and spells by 1/2/3%.

  • Mental Clarity Tier 3 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 10 points in Spellbreaker Talents
    • In the stage

    Gives your melee auto attacks a 3/5/8/11/13% chance to restore mana equal to 5 sec of your mana regeneration.

  • Deep Meditation Tier 4 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 15 points in Spellbreaker Talents
    • Requires 5 points in Mental Clarity

    Your Mental Clarity effects have a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to also deal a small amount of Arcane damage to your target.

  • Mana Weaving Tier 5 · 1 rank

    • Requires 20 points in Spellbreaker Talents
    • Requires 5 points in Deep Meditation
    • In the stage

    Each time your Deep Meditation deals damage, the cooldown of your Mana Strike is reset and your next Mana Strike costs 50% less mana.

  • Mana Charge Tier 5 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 20 points in Spellbreaker Talents
    • In the stage

    Each of your Mana Strikes that lands has a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to grant you a stack of Mana Charge, increasing the magic damage you deal by 1%. Stacks up to 8 times. Lasts 15 sec, and each new stack refreshes the duration.

  • Mirror Image Tier 5 · 1 rank

    • Requires 20 points in Spellbreaker Talents
    • In the stage

    Teaches Mirror Image.

  • Mana Breaker Tier 6 · 1 rank

    • Requires 25 points in Spellbreaker Talents
    • Requires 1 point in Mana Charge
    • In the stage

    Teaches Mana Breaker.

  • Mana Scar Tier 7 · 1 rank

    • Requires 30 points in Spellbreaker Talents
    • Requires 1 point in Mana Charge
    • In the stage

    Teaches Mana Scar.

Felblood Work in Progress 41

Work in Progress 42

Key Spells & Abilities

  • Flame Strike

    • Instant
    • Medium Mana
    • Single Target
    • Melee Range
    • Fire Damage
    • 8 sec cooldown
    • Requires Melee Weapon
    • In the stage

    An instant strike that causes 36% weapon damage plus an additional small amount as Fire damage, and sets your weapons ablaze with Burning Blades, reducing your attack speed by 5% and causing your melee auto attacks to deal an additional 10% of weapon damage as Fire damage per stack. Can be applied up to 3 times. Lasts 9 sec.

  • Immolation

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • All enemies in melee range
    • Fire Damage
    • 20 sec cooldown
    • In the stage

    Engulfs you in flames, causing a very small amount of Fire damage every 1 sec to all enemies in melee range. Lasts 10 sec.

  • Sense Demons

    • Instant
    • Self

    Shows the location of all nearby demons on the minimap until cancelled. Only one type of tracking can be used at a time.

  • Brand Prey

    • Instant
    • Low Mana
    • Single Target
    • Melee Range
    • 30 sec cooldown

    Brands the target with demon fire, increasing the damage it takes from you by 5%. Lasts 8 sec.

  • Spectral Sight

    • Instant
    • Self
    • 1 min cooldown

    Opens your spectral sight, allowing you to see all invisible and stealthed units you could detect. Lasts 8 sec.

  • Demonic Skin

    • Instant
    • Self
    • 3 min cooldown
    • In the stage

    Hardens your skin with demonic power, reducing all damage you take by a moderate amount for 15 sec.

  • Metamorphosis

    • Instant
    • Self
    • 5 min cooldown
    • In the stage

    Transforms you into a half-demon, increasing your haste by 20%, armor by 50% and health by 25%. Lasts 15 sec.

  • Eruption

    • 1 sec cast
    • High Mana
    • Single Target
    • Melee Range
    • Fire Damage
    • No cooldown
    • In the stage

    Makes the flames on your weapons erupt into the target, causing Fire damage equal to 32% of your attack power plus a large amount. Requires 3 stacks of Burning Blades, and consumes them.

Key Talents

  • Improved Flame Strike Tier 1 · 5 ranks

    Increases the damage of your Flame Strike by 5/10/15/20/25%.

  • Hatred Tier 1 · 5 ranks

    Increases your chance to get a critical strike with all attacks and spells by 1/2/3/4/5%.

  • Vengeful Parry Tier 1 · 5 ranks

    Increases your chance to parry an attack by 1/2/3/4/5%.

  • Improved Immolation Tier 2 · 2 ranks

    • Requires 5 points in Felblood Talents

    Increases the duration of your Immolation by 1/2 sec.

  • Infernal Constitution Tier 2 · 5 ranks

    • Requires 5 points in Felblood Talents

    Increases your Strength and Stamina by 1/2/3/4/5%.

  • Demonic Thirst Tier 3 · 3 ranks

    • Requires 10 points in Felblood Talents
    • In the stage

    Your Fire critical strikes restore mana equal to 1.3/2.7/4% of your base mana.

  • Infernal Release Tier 4 · 1 rank

    • Requires 15 points in Felblood Talents
    • Requires 2 points in Improved Immolation
    • In the stage

    While your Immolation is active, using it again makes it explode for 50% of the total damage it deals to one target over its full duration, increased by 5% for each second it has been active, hitting all enemies within melee range. Ends the Immolation and does not trigger the global cooldown. If the Immolation ends on its own, it does not explode.

  • Mixed Blood Tier 4 · 1 rank

    • Requires 15 points in Felblood Talents
    • In the stage

    Allows you to have two Demon Bloods active at the same time.

  • Eruption Tier 6 · 1 rank

    • Requires 25 points in Felblood Talents
    • In the stage

    Teaches Eruption.

  • Demonic Strength Tier 6 · 3 ranks

    • Requires 25 points in Felblood Talents

    Increases your attack power by an amount equal to 33/66/100% of your Strength.

  • Infernal Metamorphosis Tier 7 · 1 rank

    • Requires 30 points in Felblood Talents

    Reduces the cooldown of your Metamorphosis by 1 min, and all damage you deal while transformed becomes Fire damage.
